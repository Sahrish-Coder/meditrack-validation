
# MediTrack Validation Checklist — v0.1

## Instructions

Answer every item with Yes, No, or N/A.

- Yes must always represent a satisfactory result.
- Every checklist item must follow AOUT.
- For every No, record the relevant requirement ID and a one-line reason in the Evidence column.
- Mark uncertain answers with `?`.

## AOUT Criteria

- **A – Atomic:** One question per item.
- **O – Observable:** Can be verified by reading the SRS.
- **U – Unambiguous:** Clear and understandable.
- **T – Traceable:** Has a valid reason for inclusion.
